Beach Detector

People lose a lot of stuff at the beach. It's all still down there, some of it deep.

Sweep the detector over the sand and listen. It beeps faster as you get closer, and higher tones mean shinier metal. Weak signals are deep, so keep digging the same hole. Click to dig, click further up the beach to walk there, or use WASD.

Not everything down there is metal, and not everything down there wants to be found.

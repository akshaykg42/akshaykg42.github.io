Beach Detector

People lose a lot of things at the beach, and most of it ends up buried.

Sweep the detector over the sand. It beeps faster as you get closer, and the meter shows roughly what kind of metal it is: iron on the left, silver on the right. Hold the mouse button or Space to dig with the scoop. Walk with WASD or by clicking further up the beach, and turn with the arrow keys or by dragging with the right mouse button.

Deep targets take a few scoops. You'll also dig up things the detector can't hear, and some of what lives in the sand will run.
